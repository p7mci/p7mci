About

P7MCI

An independent Brand Identity Studio founded by Sudhan — an engineering student, brand identity designer, freelancer and creative problem solver. Every project starts by understanding the brand story before designing a memorable identity.

Engineering StudentBrand Identity DesignerFreelancerCreative Problem Solver

We believe identity work is a translation exercise — turning a founder's intent, a company's values and its audience's expectations into a visual system that holds up under real-world use, from a business card to a billboard. Design thinking, not software, drives every decision we make.

Design Philosophy

Great branding is communication, not decoration. Every logo should express purpose, trust and clarity.

Selected work

Cybersecurity

VigilantX

VigilantX

Challenge

A cybersecurity company needed to feel alert and trustworthy without leaning on tech clichés.

Creative Thinking

Rebuild the oldest symbol of protection — a shield — with sharp, deliberate geometry instead of a soft outline.

Final Identity

A geometric shield mark with a resolved checkmark, in near-black and alert yellow.

Result

A consistent identity used across product, pitch decks and hiring materials.

Education

Arohan

Arohan

Challenge

Arohan means "ascent" — the brief was to make that meaning visible at a glance.

Creative Thinking

One unbroken upward stroke, reading as a trend line, a mountain path and a graduation arc at once.

Final Identity

A confident ascending mark paired with a warm, rounded display type system.

Result

An identity carried cleanly through the platform's UI, app icon and marketing.

Hospitality

Brew Haven

Brew Haven

Challenge

A neighbourhood café wanted an identity recognisable from the cup alone.

Creative Thinking

A single cup with rising steam lines — the small pause a coffee creates in someone's day.

Final Identity

A rounded, editorial wordmark paired with a warm, handcrafted cup mark.

Result

A cohesive identity rolled out across packaging, signage and digital menus.
